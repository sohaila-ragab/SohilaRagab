02 — Assessment

Level isn’t a label. It’s what I hear you do with the language.

A 30-minute conversation. No written test — and no A1, A2 or B1 attached to a name before I’ve heard you speak.

01

The language

  • Vocabulary and range of expression

    The words you reach for, and whether you can say the same thing another way. At higher levels I’m listening for someone who moves past the beginner phrasing — not because it’s wrong, but because they have something more precise available.

    Knowing one way to say it puts you at one level. Knowing three puts you somewhere else entirely.

  • Grammar in speech

    Not whether you know the rule — whether it holds while you’re talking. How many errors, and more importantly where: in the basics, or in the advanced structures.

  • Sentence building

    How you construct a sentence, and how you connect one idea to the next.

02

The thinking

  • Thinking in German, not translating

    Whether the sentence is built in German, or assembled in another language and carried across.

  • Flow

    How far you can carry a thought before it breaks.

  • Range of topics

    Which subjects you can hold a conversation in, not just answer questions about.

  • Recovering mid-sentence

    Whether you catch a slip and carry on, or stop and lose the thread.

03

Handling the question

  • Understanding it

    Whether the question lands — without being misread, and without stalling on every single word.

  • Answering it

    Whether the answer stays on the question, or drifts to something adjacent.

  • Length

    Enough to show what you can do; not so much that it covers it up.

  • Asking back

    Whether you can ask for clarification instead of guessing.
